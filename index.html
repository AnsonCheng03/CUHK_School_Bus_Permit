  1. 落車前請按鐘；如未能按鐘，請預先通知車長。Ring the bell before alighting. If unable to do so, notify the driver in advance.
  2. 車輛停定前請留在座位或握穩扶手，上落車時請依序而行。Remain seated or hold a handrail until the bus stops, and board or alight in order.
  3. 請優先讓座予有需要人士，並避免滋擾其他乘客。Offer priority seats to passengers in need and avoid disturbing others.
  4. 請保持車門及通道暢通，並保持車廂清潔。Keep doors and aisles clear, and keep the bus clean.
